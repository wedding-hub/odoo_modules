Accounting Enterprise (Community Edition)

Replicates official Odoo Enterprise Accounting features for Odoo 20 Community Edition.

Odoo 20 Compatible OWL 2 Powered Community Edition

📊 Financial Reports

Dynamic Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, General Ledger, and Aged Payables/Receivables with interactive drilldown & PDF exports.

🏢 Fixed Asset Management

Asset models, straight-line & declining balance depreciation, prorata temporis, auto-creation from vendor bills, and bulk journal posting.

📦 Inventory & FIFO Audit

Direct reconciliation of stock.valuation.layer against GL accounts, open FIFO layer auditing, and COGS entry verification.

Installation Instructions

  1. Add /Users/lonhpiseth/odoo_modules to addons_path in your odoo.conf.
  2. Restart the Odoo 19 server.
  3. Enable Developer Mode in Odoo Settings.
  4. Go to Apps > Update Apps List and click Update.
  5. Remove the default "Apps" filter, search for account_enterprise_ce, and click Install.
